Home service owners: mistakes & fixes

Great job. Zero reviews.

Homeowners check reviews before they call. If you never ask, the happy customers stay quiet.
✗ The mistake
Finish the job, pack up, drive off and hope they leave a review.
✓ The fix: ask every time
  • 1On siteAsk face to face while they're happy with the work
  • 2Same dayText the review link with a photo of the finished job
  • 3Day 3One friendly reminder if they haven't left it
  • 4Every reviewReply to it, then use the best ones in your ads
